Most dental problems build up slowly — which is exactly why regular check-ups matter. But some symptoms are your body's way of telling you *not to wait*. If you notice any of the following, please call us rather than "seeing how it goes."

## 1. Sudden, sharp pain when biting down

This is one of the most common signs of a cracked tooth or a failing filling. Pain that appears only when you bite — and disappears the moment you stop — often means there's a fracture line transmitting pressure straight to the nerve.

## 2. A pimple-like bump on your gum

It looks harmless, almost like a small blister. It usually isn't. This is often a **dental abscess** draining through the gum tissue, and it means there is an active infection at the root of a tooth that needs treatment before it spreads.

## 3. Waking up with jaw soreness or headaches

Grinding your teeth at night (bruxism) is extremely common and often goes unnoticed until the wear starts affecting your jaw joint, your enamel, or both. A simple night-guard can prevent years of gradual damage.

## 4. Teeth that suddenly feel "loose"

Adult teeth should never feel loose. This can point to advanced gum disease (periodontitis), a bite problem, or trauma — all of which respond far better to early treatment than to waiting.

## 5. Swelling on one side of your face

Facial swelling connected to a toothache is a sign the infection may be spreading beyond the tooth itself. This is one of the few genuine dental emergencies — please call us immediately rather than waiting for a routine appointment slot.
